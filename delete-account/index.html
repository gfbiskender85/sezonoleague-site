Delete your SEZONO League account

This page explains how to delete your SEZONO League account and what happens to your data. The app is published by İskender Kalkan.

Option 1 — inside the app (instant)

  1. Open SEZONO League and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete My Account.
  3. Type your club's name to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no waiting period and it cannot be undone.

Option 2 — by e-mail (if you cannot open the app)

Write to gfbiskender85@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject “Delete my SEZONO League account”. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We answer within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

Your e-mail address, username, password hash, Apple/Google sign-in link, date of birth, sessions, notifications and push tokens, manager profile, scouting and observation notes, mastery records, wage history, club seat, ad reward records and your account itself.

What is kept, and why

  • Your club is not deleted. It plays in a live league that other managers depend on. It is handed over to an AI manager and its link to you is cut.
  • Game-world records (match results, transfers, trophies) remain, no longer connected to you.
  • Purchase records are kept de-identified for as long as tax and bookkeeping law requires (in Türkiye, as a rule 10 years).
  • Consent records are kept de-identified, because we must be able to prove that consent was given.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription held with an app store. Cancel it in your store account's subscription settings.

More details: Privacy Policy, section 9.
