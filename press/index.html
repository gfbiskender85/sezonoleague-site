Description

One line

A football manager game with one living world, where every rival in your league is another person.

Short (50 words)

SEZONO League is a football manager game with a single shared world. You sign as a manager, not as the owner of a club: build the eleven, work the market, read the opponent. Every club in your league is run by another person, and the season runs on real days.

Long (120 words)

Most manager games hand you a private universe that pauses when you leave. SEZONO League runs one world for everyone. Your division is filled with real managers on the real calendar — transfer windows open and close while you sleep, and the table you climb is the same table they are climbing.

You own a career rather than a club: your profile, your licence and every result follow you from one desk to the next. Matches are simulated with a tactical engine you can read, not a dice roll: shape, condition, morale and the captain's armband all leave fingerprints on the result.

Season 1 opens on 18 October 2026. A closed test runs before launch on iOS and Android.

What makes it different

  • One living world. No private save file — same leagues, same market, same table for everyone.
  • Real days. The season follows the calendar; a window that closes tonight is closed tomorrow.
  • A career, not a club. Licence levels, job offers, a board that can sack you.
  • Money buys the stage, never the score. Paying changes what your club looks like, not what it wins.
"Every club in that table is run by a real manager, on real days. No scripted ladder, no AI filler."
